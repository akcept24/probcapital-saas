Risk & anti-abuse

Every block comes with a reason

Black-box bans are how firms earn chargebacks and Reddit threads. Our engine records the rule, the numbers and the trades behind every decision — evidence you can show the trader, your PSP, or a court.

A reason on every action

Each breach, block or payout hold is stored with the exact rule, the measured values and the timestamp. No "violation of our terms" dead ends.

Copy & hedge detection

Mirrored entries, identical timing and opposite positions across accounts are flagged as one scheme — not as isolated coincidences.

An evidence trail per account

The full decision history of an account — trades, rule checks, actions taken — in one exportable record, built for dispute resolution.

The trader sees it too

The dashboard shows the rule and the numbers behind a decision. Traders who understand a block don't charge back — they adjust.
