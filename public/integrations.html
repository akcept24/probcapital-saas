Integrations

Connect your stack.
Keep your money flow.

ProbCapital plugs into the providers your firm already uses — payments, KYC, trading platforms and notifications. Your PSP keys, your provider accounts, your data. Money never passes through us.

PaymentsPSP & payment webhooks

Traders pay on your branded checkout through your own PSP account. The moment a payment lands, the PSP webhook provisions the challenge account automatically — no manual activation, no copy-pasting.

Trader pays→PSP webhook→Account provisioned→Credentials emailed
  • Live today: Plisio (crypto) and Stripe (cards)
  • Your keys: deposits and payouts settle to your accounts, never to ProbCapital
  • Another PSP? if it speaks webhooks, we wire it during onboarding

ComplianceTrader KYC providers

Identity verification runs inside the trader dashboard before the first payout — through your own account with the provider you choose.

  • Sumsub — full KYC/AML flows
  • Veriff — document & biometric verification
  • Shufti Pro — global document coverage
  • Verification status is visible to the trader and to your risk team

TradingTerminal & MetaTrader

Our proprietary terminal is the default trading surface — web and mobile layout, live quotes from our Quote Hub. If your traders live in MetaTrader, those accounts sit next to it in the same system.

  • Proprietary terminal: your brand, charts, one-click trading
  • MT4 / MT5: accounts created and synced — balance and equity visible in the same dashboard
  • Quote Hub: one feed for terminal, risk engine and leaderboards

OperationsEmail & Telegram alerts

The platform talks to traders and to your team in your brand's voice.

  • Transactional email: receipts, account credentials, payout status — under your name
  • Telegram ops alerts: new registrations, payout requests and risk events land in your team chat in real time — this is how our own firm runs
  • Support tickets built into the trader dashboard

Your dataExport & exit

Your traders, accounts, trades, payouts and audit logs are yours — structurally, not by promise.

  • Full export of accounts, trades, payouts and audit history
  • No hostage clauses: if you ever leave, we help you migrate away
  • Nightly backups of the platform database
