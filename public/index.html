03 · Risk & anti-abuse

Every block carries a reason

A block with no explanation becomes a chargeback and a forum thread. Our engine stores the rule, the measured values and the trades behind each decision: evidence you can show the trader, your payment provider, or a court.

a.

A reason on every action

Each breach, block or payout hold is stored with the exact rule, the measured values and the timestamp. No "violation of our terms" dead ends.

b.

Copy & hedge detection

Mirrored entries, identical timing and opposite positions across accounts are flagged as one scheme.

c.

An evidence trail per account

The full decision history of an account (trades, rule checks, actions taken) in one exportable record, built for dispute resolution.

d.

The trader sees it too

The dashboard shows the rule and the numbers behind a decision. When a trader can see both, support gets a question instead of a chargeback.

Sample block record

Rule Copy-trading: mirrored execution across accounts
Accounts 3 accounts, 41 matched orders over 6 days
Evidence Same instruments, same direction, median entry delta 0.8s
Action Payout hold. Full record available to the firm and the trader
